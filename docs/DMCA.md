# Copyright and takedown policy

EmblemWeave is an open-source interoperability project. It does not operate a
user-uploaded content service, does not provide game files, keys, tickets,
decrypted game content or circumvention instructions, and does not accept game
data through its issue tracker or contributions.

## Where the project is hosted

The canonical repository and releases are hosted on GitHub. Source mirrors are
hosted on GitLab and GitGud, and the project site has a static fallback page.
Those providers control their own hosting and publish their own copyright and
abuse processes.

## Reporting copyright infringement

If you own or control a copyright and believe material in a repository, release
or project page infringes it:

1. **Use the hosting provider's process first.** For GitHub, use
   [GitHub's DMCA process](https://github.com/contact/dmca). For GitLab or
   GitGud, use the copyright or abuse process published by that host. The host
   can remove or disable access to material it stores, and it is the correct
   recipient for a formal DMCA notice about hosted content.
2. **Notify the maintainer as well**, through the repository's **Security** tab
   as a private advisory or the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub
   profile. A complete notice should identify:
   - the copyrighted work and the specific URL or file you say infringes it;
   - your name, address, telephone number and email address;
   - a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by
     the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
   - a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice
     is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act
     for the owner;
   - your physical or electronic signature.

   These are the elements of a formal notice under 17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3); a host
   may require its own form. Do not send game files, keys, personal data or
   material you do not have the right to share.

## What the project will do

For material the maintainer controls, we will review a complete notice, remove
or disable access to material that should not be there, and tell the contributor
where appropriate. We may ask the hosting provider to act on material the
provider stores. We will not remove material merely because a notice is
incomplete or disputed; we will follow the host's process and applicable law.

If material was removed and you believe it was removed by mistake or
misidentification, use the hosting provider's counter-notice process and provide
the information that process requires. The project will follow the host's
process and applicable law; it does not decide ownership disputes.

## No safe-harbor claim

EmblemWeave does not operate a service that hosts user-uploaded content and does
not claim protection under 17 U.S.C. §512. GitHub, GitLab and GitGud operate the
hosting services and handle notices for content they host. This page describes
the project's own review policy; it is not legal advice, and it does not create
rights or obligations beyond those in applicable law.

## Repeat infringement

Because the project has no user-uploaded content or account system, it has no
account-based repeat-infringer system. Material found to infringe is removed,
and the maintainer may decline or block further contributions from a repeat
infringer.
