## Upscaler files

FSR 3 (AMD), XeSS (Intel) and DLSS (NVIDIA) need the vendors' own DLLs, which are not part of this project and are not in this repository
or its releases. When you press a Download button in the launcher's Settings, the launcher shows the vendor's licence terms, asks you to
accept them, and then downloads the files from the vendor's own GitHub repository (AMD's FidelityFX-SDK, Intel's xess, NVIDIA's
Streamline release) to your PC, checking each against a pinned SHA-256. The project does not host, mirror or modify them. They stay the
property of AMD, Intel and NVIDIA and are yours to use only under those companies' terms. NVIDIA's DLSS terms in particular are not an
open-source licence; read them before accepting. None of these companies is affiliated with or endorses this project.

Rights holders can reach the maintainer by opening an issue titled "Rights holder contact" (no details needed in public; we will reply
with a private channel) or through GitHub's DMCA process.
